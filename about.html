OUR STORY

About CrestPoint

Fourteen years of putting the paperwork before the photographs.

HOW WE STARTED

Built on one bad transaction.

CrestPoint was founded in 2012 after our founder watched a family friend lose a deposit on a Lekki plot that turned out to have three separate claims of ownership. The agency that sold it had never checked. CrestPoint was built to be the agency that always does.

Today we operate from two offices — Victoria Island and Abuja — with forty agents and a legal team that reviews every title before it reaches a listing page. We've sold over 500 properties. We have never once skipped the search.

A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDER

"We are not the fastest agency in Lagos, and we've stopped apologising for it. Every listing on this site has been through a title search a client will never see the cost of. That's the job."
