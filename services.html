WHAT WE DO

Services

Six disciplines, one point of contact. From a single sale to managing an entire portfolio.

01

Property Sales

Verified residential listings across Lagos and Abuja — from serviced apartments to waterfront mansions, each screened for clean title before it's shown to a client.

02

Land Sales

Surveyed, titled plots in growth corridors like Sangotedo, Epe, and Guzape, sold with the survey plan and title documents disclosed upfront.

03

Property Development

End-to-end development management for landowners — design, approvals, construction oversight, and eventual sale or lease-up.

04

Property Management

Rent collection, tenant screening, maintenance coordination, and reporting for landlords who'd rather not do it themselves. Over 300 units currently under management.

05

Facility Management

Estate security, power, cleaning, and grounds contracts for residential compounds and commercial buildings across our coverage areas.

06

Real Estate Investment

Advisory on rental yield, capital appreciation, and exit timing — plus direct access to off-market and pre-launch inventory.
