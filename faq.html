COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ

The questions we get asked before every single closing.

How do I know a property's title is genuine?

Every CrestPoint listing has been through a title search by our in-house legal team before it's published — Certificate of Occupancy, Governor's Consent, or a verified deed of assignment. We share the search report with serious buyers on request.

Do you charge a fee to buyers?

Our standard agency commission is paid by the seller. Buyers only pay for their own legal fees and any documentation charges, which are disclosed upfront before any offer is made.

Can I inspect a property before committing?

Yes — every listing includes a free physical or virtual inspection, arranged around your schedule with an agent present throughout.

What payment plans are available?

Most properties follow a three-stage plan: deposit on offer acceptance, a second payment on completion of documentation, and the balance on handover. Land purchases often support 6–12 month instalments. Exact terms vary by listing.

Do you manage rental properties for landlords?

Yes — our property management division currently handles over 300 units, covering tenant screening, rent collection, maintenance, and monthly reporting.

Do you sell property outside Lagos and Abuja?

Our core coverage is Lagos and Abuja, but we occasionally facilitate sales in other states through partner agencies. Ask your agent if you have a specific location in mind.

How fast can a sale close?

Our fastest closing was eleven days, start to finish. Most straightforward sales close within 4–6 weeks, largely dependent on documentation.
